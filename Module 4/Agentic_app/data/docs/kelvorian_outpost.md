# Kelvorian Outpost — Site Profile

The Kelvorian Outpost is located in the fictional Kelvorian mountain range. It was established (fictionally) in the year 2089.

## People and shifts
The outpost houses a crew of 46. Crew work in three 8-hour shifts, and each crew member rotates out after a 90-day tour.

## Supplies
Supply drones arrive every 12 days. The outpost keeps a reserve of 30 days of food and 45 days of water in case a drone is delayed.

## Access
The only ground route is the Everlore Pass, which closes from November to March because of snow. During those months all traffic arrives by air.

## Communications
The outpost's uplink runs through the Orbital Relay Station. Field teams carry Zylarian Communicators.
