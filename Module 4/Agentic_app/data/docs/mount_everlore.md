# Mount Everlore — Survey Notes

Mount Everlore is 8,850 meters tall and is located in the fictional Kelvorian range. It is the highest peak in the range.

## Climbing
The standard route takes 9 days from base camp to summit and back. Base camp sits at 5,200 meters, which leaves 3,650 meters of ascent to the summit.

## Weather
The summit temperature averages -36 degrees Celsius in winter. Climbing permits are issued only from April to June.

## Relation to the outpost
The Kelvorian Outpost lies 22 kilometres east of the mountain's base camp, on the far side of the Everlore Pass.
