# Orbital Relay Station — Operations Brief

The Orbital Relay Station has 340 units of fuel reserve and consumes 28 units per day of normal operation.

## Operating modes
In normal operation the station burns 28 units of fuel per day. In low-power mode it burns 12 units per day, but message throughput drops by half. In emergency boost mode it burns 55 units per day.

## Refuelling
A refuelling tanker can deliver up to 400 units per trip. Tankers must be scheduled at least 6 days in advance.

## Crew and orbit
The station is crewed by 5 operators and orbits at an altitude of 36,000 kilometres. It relays traffic for the Kelvorian Outpost and for Zylarian Communicators that are out of direct range.
