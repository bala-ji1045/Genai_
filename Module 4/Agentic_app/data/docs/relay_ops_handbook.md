# Relay Ops Handbook — Policies

## Incident priority levels
Incidents are graded P1 to P3. P1 (critical, for example a relay outage) needs a response within 1 hour. P2 (high) needs a response within 4 hours. P3 (normal) needs a response within 24 hours.

## On-call
For P1 incidents, page the on-call engineer through the #relay-incidents channel. The on-call rota changes every Monday at 09:00 station time.

## Reports
Operational reports go to the ops lead. Any email sent outside the team must be approved by a human before it is sent.

## Equipment returns
Faulty Zylarian Communicators can be returned within 30 days for a replacement. Units marked "field-damaged" cannot be returned.
