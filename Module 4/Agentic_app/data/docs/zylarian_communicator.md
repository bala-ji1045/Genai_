# Zylarian Communicator — Product Sheet

The Zylarian Communicator is a compact interstellar comm device, released in a fictional product line for this course.

## Power
It has a 3,200 mAh battery. On a full charge it runs for about 40 hours of standby or 9 hours of continuous transmission. A full recharge from empty takes 2 hours on the standard dock.

## Hardware
The device weighs 210 grams and has a 2.8-inch transflective display that stays readable in direct starlight. The casing is rated to work between -40 and +65 degrees Celsius.

## Range and links
Line-of-sight range is 1,200 kilometres. Beyond that, messages hop through the Orbital Relay Station, which adds roughly 4 seconds of latency per hop.

## Warranty
Every unit carries a 24-month warranty. Water damage is not covered.
